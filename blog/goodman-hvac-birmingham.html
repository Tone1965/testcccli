Maintenance Requirements for Goodman Longevity

Like all HVAC equipment, Goodman systems require regular maintenance to deliver optimal performance and longevity. Birmingham's demanding climate makes maintenance especially important, as systems work harder and longer than in many parts of the country. Goodman recommends professional maintenance twice per year: before cooling season begins (typically March or April) and before heating season starts (September or October).

Professional maintenance visits include thorough cleaning of indoor and outdoor coils to maintain heat transfer efficiency, checking and adjusting refrigerant charge to manufacturer specifications, inspecting and tightening all electrical connections, testing capacitors and other electrical components, lubricating motors and moving parts as needed, cleaning or replacing air filters, inspecting ductwork for leaks or damage, testing thermostat accuracy and calibration, verifying proper airflow throughout the system, and inspecting safety controls and shut-off devices.

Regular maintenance prevents small issues from becoming expensive repairs, maintains energy efficiency so your utility bills stay manageable, preserves warranty coverage which often requires proof of professional maintenance, and extends system lifespan by reducing wear and preventing premature failures. For Birmingham homeowners, the modest investment in biannual maintenance typically pays for itself through improved efficiency and fewer emergency repairs.
