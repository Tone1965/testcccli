## H2: Common HVAC Emergencies We Handle

### Recognizing When You Need Emergency Service

Not every HVAC problem requires emergency service, but certain situations demand immediate attention to prevent property damage, health hazards, or complete system failure.

**Call for Emergency Service If You Experience:**

1. **Complete Loss of Heating or Cooling During Extreme Weather**
   - No heat when temperatures drop below 40°F
   - No AC when temperatures exceed 85°F with high humidity

2. **Safety Concerns**
   - Gas odors near your furnace
   - Burning smells or visible smoke
   - Suspected carbon monoxide issues
   - Electrical sparking or burning wire smell
   - Water leaking near electrical components

3. **Unusual Noises Indicating Imminent Failure**
   - Loud banging or explosion sounds
   - Metal-on-metal grinding
   - Continuous clicking or buzzing from electrical components

4. **System Short Cycling**
   - HVAC turns on and off every few minutes
   - Repeatedly trips circuit breakers
   - Can't maintain set temperature despite constant running

5. **Refrigerant Leaks**
   - Hissing sounds from refrigerant lines
   - Ice buildup on outdoor unit in summer
   - Sudden loss of cooling capacity

6. **High-Priority Business Situations**
   - Restaurant walk-in cooler failure
   - Medical facility climate control loss
   - Data center cooling system problems
   - Senior care facility heating or cooling loss

## H2: Preventing HVAC Emergencies - Maintenance Tips

### Proactive Care Reduces Emergency Calls

While our team is always ready for your emergency, the best HVAC emergency is the one that never happens. Regular maintenance can prevent up to 85% of HVAC failures.

**Seasonal Maintenance Checklist:**

**Spring (Before Cooling Season):**
- Replace air filters (or clean if reusable)
- Clear debris from outdoor condenser unit
- Check refrigerant levels
- Test thermostat accuracy
- Inspect electrical connections
- Clean evaporator and condenser coils

**Fall (Before Heating Season):**
- Inspect heat exchanger for cracks
- Test ignition system and safety controls
- Verify proper gas pressure and burner operation
- Check venting and exhaust systems
- Lubricate moving parts
- Test carbon monoxide detectors

**Monthly Tasks:**
- Check and replace air filters as needed
- Ensure all vents and registers remain unobstructed
- Listen for unusual noises during operation
- Monitor energy bills for unexpected increases

## H2: What Makes an HVAC Situation an Emergency?

### When to Call vs. When to Schedule Regular Service

**TRUE EMERGENCIES - Call Immediately:**
- Any safety concern (gas smell, smoke, sparking)
- Complete system failure during temperature extremes
- Water leaking near electrical components
- Suspected carbon monoxide issues
- Loud catastrophic noises indicating imminent failure

**URGENT BUT NOT EMERGENCY - Call Same Day:**
- System running but not maintaining comfortable temperature
- System short cycling but still providing some heating/cooling
- Unusual noises that develop gradually
- Higher-than-normal energy bills
- Inconsistent temperatures between rooms

**ROUTINE ISSUES - Schedule Regular Service:**
- Annual maintenance and tune-ups
- Filter replacements and cleaning
- Efficiency upgrades
- Thermostat programming assistance
- Ductwork inspection and sealing

When in doubt, call us. Our team can help you determine the urgency of your situation and schedule appropriate service. We'd rather assess a situation and find it's not an emergency than have you wait and experience system failure.
